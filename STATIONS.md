# Music station sources

Lofi Focus plays the broadcasters' public streams directly. It does not host or
redistribute their recordings. No additional player, account or API key is
needed for these streams.

## Lo-fi radio

| Station | Music | Operator's ad policy and source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Lilo-Fi Radio](https://lilofiradio.com/lofi-music-no-ads) | Lo-fi hip-hop | The operator states that its own station has no ads, jingles or mid-stream interruptions. This does not cover other stations in the Lilo-Fi app. |
| [Kalizo Lo-fi](https://www.kalizoradio.com/radio/lofi/) | Mellow lo-fi, soft jazz and ambient textures | Formerly Chillofi Radio. Its official page describes continuous music without presenters or advertising breaks and publishes the direct MP3 URL. |
| [Purrple Cat](https://live365.com/station/Purrple-Cat---No-Ads-a30440) | Dreamy instrumental lo-fi by Purrple Cat | The artist's station page on Live365 explicitly describes this stream as ad-free with zero commercials. The URL comes from that station's public player. |
| [Lofi Cafe · Chilling](https://loficafe.net/chilling) | Slow lo-fi hip-hop, mellow beats and jazz instrumentals | The operator states no ads or interruptions. The URL is the direct stream used by its official web player. |

The three newer streams were checked with real mpv playback using null audio
output before inclusion. A playback check establishes that a URL works at that
time; it cannot guarantee continuous availability or audit every future track.
Ad-free descriptions reflect the operators' published policies, checked on
2026-09-24. If a broadcaster changes its policy or inserts ads, please report
which station and approximately when it happened so it can be removed.

SomaFM is no longer included. Its [terms updated on 2026-09-04](https://somafm.com/contact/tos.html)
explicitly state that new third-party applications cannot be authorized to use
its streams. The bundled music selection now consists of the four lo-fi
stations above.

These music policies do not apply to the optional podcasts, talk radio or ATC
channels. Those are separate third-party broadcasts and may contain speech or
sponsor messages. Offline nature sounds and their licenses are documented in
[SOUNDS-LICENSES.md](SOUNDS-LICENSES.md).
